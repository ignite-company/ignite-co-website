Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the website at www.igniteco.org and related communications from Ignite Co., operated by Ignite Company LLC ("Ignite," "we," or "us"). We provide marketing, lead response, customer relationship management, and business automation services.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us or request a consultation, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, company name, email address, telephone number, industry, messages, and appointment details. We may also receive technical information such as your device type, referring page, site interactions, and general usage or analytics information collected through cookies and comparable technologies.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, assess whether our services are relevant to your business, coordinate consultations, provide customer support, manage customer relationships, maintain security, and improve the website and our services. We may use analytics and measurement tools to understand website usage.

4. SMS messaging and your consent

If you actively check the optional SMS consent box on our contact form or another disclosed booking form, Ignite Company LLC may send non-marketing messages related to your requested consultation, including booking confirmations, reminders, scheduling changes, and replies to your appointment questions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to end messages or HELP for support. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of submitting an inquiry or obtaining services.

Providing a phone number alone does not mean you have opted into text messaging. We use your recorded SMS preferences to determine whether you can receive these messages.

5. Mobile information and sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. We may use vendors acting as service providers to support our operations, including hosting, form delivery, customer service, scheduling, and communications, under instructions to process information for our services. We do not share text messaging opt-in data or consent with third parties for their own marketing or promotional use.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our site may use cookies, pixels, or similar technologies for analytics, measurement, functionality, and site performance. Browser settings may allow you to limit cookies. Disabling certain technologies may affect some features.

7. Retention and security

We keep inquiry and business communication records for as long as reasonably needed to fulfill their purposes, manage relationships, comply with legal requirements, and resolve disputes. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You can ask us about the information we hold about you or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs. To stop SMS messages, reply STOP to any Ignite text. You can also contact us about privacy matters by email.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our practices change. The effective date above reflects the latest posted version. For questions, contact Ignite Company LLC by email at asher.igniteco@gmail.com or telephone at (201) 973-9748. Business address: 131 Main St., Hackensack, NJ.
