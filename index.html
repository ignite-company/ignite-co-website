How is this estimated?

We model three separate groups to avoid counting the same inquiry twice: (1) previously unreached inquiries, assuming 45% become reachable with faster response and then close at your stated rate; (2) reached but unbooked inquiries, assuming an additional 7%, 4.5% or 1.5% book through improved follow-up when your current follow-up is rare, occasional or consistent; (3) past customers who are not counted among new inquiries, assuming 3.5% book again through reactivation. Time savings assume 65% of the follow-up hours you entered. These percentages are modeling assumptions rather than verified Ignite Co. historical conversion rates. Estimates are gross revenue before costs, with no guarantee of results.
