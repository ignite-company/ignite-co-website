GOOD QUESTIONS

Built around
your operation.

You're buying a better business process, not just another platform.

Do we have to replace the tools we already use?

Not necessarily. We start with your existing systems and identify what can be improved or connected. Replacements are recommended only when they make sense for your business.

Do we need all six solutions?

No. Your buildout is based on the gaps worth fixing first. Some companies need better first response, others need stronger estimate follow-up or a cleaner CRM.

Will we own the systems you build?

Account setup, access, ownership, and any required third-party subscriptions are agreed during scoping. We prioritize business-controlled accounts and practical handover wherever the platforms allow it.

Can Ignite help manage the systems afterward?

Yes, ongoing support and management can be included as an optional, separately scoped service. We'll clarify what maintenance is involved before launch.
