WHY THE SYSTEM MATTERS

More leads won't fix a broken sales process.

A contractor can have a strong website and still lose work. A homeowner fills out a form at night, the office calls back days later, a consultation never gets scheduled, or a detailed proposal disappears into a crowded inbox.

That's why our approach to home improvement marketing includes the steps after the click: lead qualification, rapid notification, booking, estimate tracking, and thoughtful follow-up. Your team still makes the personal decisions. The systems make those decisions easier to carry out consistently.

Instead of recommending more advertising by default, we first look for the stage where real customer opportunities are getting stuck.

FROM THE FIRST CLICK TO A SIGNED JOB
  1. A homeowner finds your business online or by referral
  2. The right service page explains what you do
  3. A simple inquiry captures project details
  4. Your team follows up and qualifies the request
  5. A call, estimate, or consultation gets booked
  6. Open opportunities stay visible until resolved
  7. Past customers receive relevant post-job outreach

The handoffs matter as much as the traffic source.

How we're different from lead-only marketing

We ask what happens after the form submission, not just how many forms were submitted.

  • Which inquiries fit your services and service area?
  • How long does a homeowner wait for a response?
  • Who owns the next step?
  • How many qualified inquiries become consultations?
  • Which estimates are still awaiting a decision?

Those answers help identify whether the next investment should be in acquisition, conversion, or operational follow-through.

HOW WE APPROACH A BUILDOUT

Understand the business before recommending the tools.

  1. Map the customer journey. Identify where inquiries enter, who owns them, and how jobs get booked today.
  2. Find the conversion gaps. Review lead response, qualification, appointments, pending estimates, and lost opportunities.
  3. Scope the highest-value improvements. Decide whether you need landing pages, a CRM, workflow automations, campaigns, or a combination.
  4. Implement around your team. Connect appropriate tools, define responsibilities, and make the process usable day to day.
  5. Track outcomes. Measure qualified inquiries, bookings, estimate decisions, and customer follow-up so you can improve over time.

Measure the work that matters

Qualified lead rate: share of inquiries that fit your service, area, and project criteria.

Consultation booking rate: consultations booked divided by qualified inquiries.

Estimate close rate: signed projects divided by estimates sent.

Marketing cost per signed project: spend divided by projects attributed to that marketing.

Results depend on your market, offer, team, and budget. No particular number of jobs or ranking is guaranteed.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Choosing a home improvement marketing agency.

What should a home improvement marketing agency actually do?

It should help generate suitable inquiries, clearly communicate your services, and track how those inquiries become estimates and signed work. Depending on its scope, it may also help with advertising, websites, CRM systems, and sales follow-up.

Do I need to buy more ads to get more projects?

Not necessarily. If leads are already coming in but responses or estimates are falling through the cracks, improving conversion may be the better first investment.

Does Ignite Co. work with kitchen and bathroom remodelers?

Yes. We help residential remodeling companies connect their inquiries, consultations, estimate process, and customer follow-up. The approach is tailored to project scope and sales cycle.

Will I have to replace my CRM or scheduling platform?

Not automatically. We review your existing setup before recommending what to improve, integrate, or replace. Account ownership and any ongoing platform costs are clarified during scoping.

How quickly can organic SEO generate remodeling leads?

Organic visibility develops over time and depends on site quality, competition, useful content, and your authority in the market. Indexing a new page does not guarantee rankings or a specific number of inquiries.
